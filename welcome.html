Your crystal ritual

Your bracelet arrives cleansed and charged. These simple rituals help you keep its energy clear and connected to you.

Set your intention

Do this before you wear your bracelet for the first time, and whenever your goals change.

  1. Sit quietly and hold your bracelet in both palms.
  2. Close your eyes and take three slow, deep breaths.
  3. Think of what you wish to invite into your life.
  4. Say it softly, as if it is already true. For example: "I am calm and clear."
  5. Wear it on your left wrist to receive its energy.

Cleanse your crystals

Crystals absorb the energy around them. Cleanse your bracelet whenever it feels heavy, after a difficult day, or once a month. Choose whichever way feels right for you:

  • Sacred smoke: light sage, dhoop, loban or incense and gently pass your bracelet through the smoke a few times, holding the intention that it is cleared.
  • Sound: ring a bell or a singing bowl close to your bracelet for a minute. The vibration clears stagnant energy.
  • Breath: hold it close to your lips and blow on it gently three times with a clear intention.

Avoid cleansing with water or salt. Some stones can lose their shine, and the elastic weakens.

Charge your crystals

After cleansing, refill your bracelet with fresh energy:

  • Moonlight: leave it on a windowsill overnight, especially on a full moon night.
  • Selenite plate: rest it on a selenite plate overnight. Selenite gently clears and recharges any crystal placed on it.
  • Crystal cluster: rest it on a clear quartz cluster or amethyst geode for a few hours.
  • Your intention: hold it, repeat your intention, and imagine golden light filling every bead.

Keep your bracelet out of direct sunlight. Stones like amethyst, citrine and rose quartz can fade in the sun.

Care for your bracelet

  • Remove it before bathing, swimming or exercising.
  • Put on perfume, lotion and creams before wearing it, never after.
  • Roll it onto your wrist instead of stretching it, so the elastic lasts.
  • When you are not wearing it, keep it in its MyMA pouch.
