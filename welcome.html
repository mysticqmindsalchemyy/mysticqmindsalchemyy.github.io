Your crystal ritual

Your bracelet arrives cleansed and charged. Tap each ritual to open it.

❂When your bracelet arrives

Before you wear it for the first time, welcome it into your energy:

  1. Hold your bracelet in your folded palms for a few moments and set a clear intention (see "Set your intention" below).
  2. Gently pass it through sage, dhoop or incense smoke.
  3. Wear it with the belief that the intention it has been programmed with is already working for you.
✦Set your intention

Whenever you set or renew your intention:

  1. Sit quietly and hold your bracelet in both palms.
  2. Close your eyes and take three slow, deep breaths.
  3. Think of what you wish to invite into your life.
  4. Say it softly, as if it is already true. For example: "I am calm and clear."
  5. Wear it on your left wrist to receive its energy.
☾Cleanse your crystals

Crystals absorb the energy around them. Cleanse your bracelet when it feels heavy, after a difficult day, or once a month:

  • Sacred smoke: pass it through sage, dhoop, loban or incense smoke with the intention that it is cleared.
  • Sound: ring a bell or singing bowl close to it for a minute.
  • Breath: hold it near your lips and blow on it gently three times.

Avoid water and salt. Some stones lose their shine, and the elastic weakens.

✧Charge your crystals
  • Selenite plate: rest it on a selenite plate overnight. Selenite gently clears and recharges any crystal placed on it.
  • Moonlight: leave it on a windowsill overnight, especially on a full moon.
  • Crystal cluster: rest it on a clear quartz cluster or amethyst geode for a few hours.
  • Your intention: hold it, repeat your intention, and imagine golden light filling every bead.

Keep it out of direct sunlight. Amethyst, citrine and rose quartz can fade in the sun.

❀Care for your bracelet
  • Remove it before bathing, swimming or exercising.
  • Put on perfume and lotion before wearing it, never after.
  • Roll it onto your wrist instead of stretching it.
  • When not wearing it, keep it in its MyMA pouch.
