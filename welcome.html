Caring for Your Crystals

Simple rituals to keep their energy strong

Set your intention

Whenever you set or renew your intention:

  • Sit quietly and hold your crystals in both palms.
  • Close your eyes and take three slow, deep breaths.
  • Think of what you wish to invite into your life.
  • Say it softly, as if it is already true. For example: “I am calm and clear.”
  • Wear each piece as guided in your letter above.

Cleanse your crystals

Crystals absorb the energy around them. Cleanse them when they feel heavy, after a difficult day, or once a month:

  • Sacred smoke: gently pass them through smoke with the intention that they are cleared.
  • Sound: ring a bell or singing bowl close to them for a minute.

Avoid water and salt. Some stones lose their shine, and the elastic weakens.

Charge your crystals

  • Selenite plate: rest them on a selenite plate for 3–4 hours.
  • Moonlight: leave them in moonlight on a windowsill for 3–4 hours, especially on a full moon.
  • Crystal cluster: rest them on a clear quartz cluster or amethyst geode for 3–4 hours.
  • Your intention: hold them, repeat your intention, and imagine golden light filling every bead.

Keep them out of direct sunlight. Amethyst, citrine and rose quartz can fade in the sun.

Everyday care

  • Remove them before bathing, swimming or exercising.
  • Put on perfume and lotion before wearing them, never after.
  • Roll bracelets onto your wrist instead of stretching them.
  • When not wearing them, keep them in a clean space where no one else touches them.

Good to know

  • Natural stones, natural marks: because every MyMA crystal is natural, tiny inclusions, lines or marks are part of its character. They are a sign of a real stone, not damage.
  • Authentically sourced: each crystal is sourced from authentic origins and checked with care before it reaches you.
  • Who can wear them: anyone can wear crystals. There is no need for kundali matching or any special timing.
  • Your effort still matters: a crystal supports your intention, but it does not replace your own action. Wear it, work towards what you want, and let its energy walk beside you.
  • If a bracelet breaks by accident: if it snaps because it caught on something or was pulled, gather the beads (if you can find them) and simply restring them. Please avoid wearing a broken or damaged piece.
  • If it breaks all of a sudden: do not worry. In crystal traditions, a crystal that breaks on its own is often seen as having absorbed heavy energy for you and completed its purpose. Gently gather the beads, thank them, and either return them to the earth by placing them in soil or a plant pot, or keep them in a clean, sacred space.
  • Need new crystals? Once a crystal has broken or completed its purpose, a replacement is a fresh piece, chosen and energised anew for you, and is ordered as a new piece.
