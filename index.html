Tapashree Mutsuddy

My journey into the mystical arts began long before I ever gave it a name.

With over 17 years of corporate experience in client handling, stakeholder management, and IT delivery, my professional life was rooted in logic, structure, and performance. I hold a Master's degree from BITS Pilani, Hyderabad, along with a specialization in Business Strategy and Formulation from the Indian School of Business.

Yet, beneath this structured world, I was always deeply connected to something more.

From a young age, I was sensitive to the energies around me — drawn to the patterns in numbers, the stories in the stars, and the wisdom hidden in ancient systems.

Over time, I began to notice a common thread — despite success and constant motion, many felt stuck, emotionally overwhelmed, and unclear about their path. And I understood why.

Clarity doesn't come from doing more — it comes from understanding yourself at a deeper level.

After years of personal transformation and inner exploration, I founded Mysticq Minds Alchemyy with a clear purpose:

✨ To help you decode your unique blueprint
✨ To align your energy with your true path
✨ To gain clarity in moments of confusion — because every soul is here with a purpose
✨ And to step fully into your personal power

My approach blends both logic and intuition — combining the precision of Numerology, the intuitive wisdom of Tarot, the transformational depth of coaching, and the healing energy of Reiki.

Because I truly believe — every soul is here with a purpose. And with the right guidance, even the most uncertain crossroads can become a path of transformation.
