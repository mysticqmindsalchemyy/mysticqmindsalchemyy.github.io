Your weekly guidance for the closing days of **Pitru Paksha**. Find your Mulank below to discover your card for the week, the energy it brings, and gentle guidance for the days ahead.

![This week's tarot spread for Mulank 1 to 9](/assets/img/weekly/2026-10-04-spread.jpg)

## Mulank 1 — The Star

*Ruled by Sun · Surya*

**This week's energy:** After a season of effort, a soft light of hope returns. Healing is quietly flowing in, and you can feel lighter again.

**Guidance:** Trust that the blessings of your ancestors are guiding you. Rest, renew, and let yourself believe in good things.

## Mulank 2 — The Hermit

*Ruled by Moon · Chandra*

**This week's energy:** Your heart is asking for stillness. This is a beautiful week to step back from the noise and listen to your inner voice.

**Guidance:** Give yourself quiet time through prayer, meditation or a peaceful walk. The answers you seek are already glowing within you.

## Mulank 3 — Page of Wands

*Ruled by Jupiter · Guru*

**This week's energy:** A fresh spark of curiosity is lighting up inside you. A new idea, message or opportunity may arrive and excite you.

**Guidance:** Explore it playfully, without pressure to have it all figured out. Let your enthusiasm lead the way.

## Mulank 4 — Ten of Pentacles

*Ruled by Rahu*

**This week's energy:** Family, roots and legacy are beautifully highlighted. What your elders built continues to support and protect you.

**Guidance:** Honour your lineage with gratitude this Pitru Paksha. Small acts of remembrance can bring deep blessings to your home.

## Mulank 5 — King of Cups

*Ruled by Mercury · Budh*

**This week's energy:** Even if life moves quickly around you, you have the power to stay calm and kind. Your emotional strength is your gift this week.

**Guidance:** Respond from a peaceful heart rather than react. Your steady presence will comfort the people around you.

## Mulank 6 — The Magician

*Ruled by Venus · Shukra*

**This week's energy:** You have everything you need to create something beautiful. Your words, talents and intentions carry real power right now.

**Guidance:** Set one clear, loving intention and take a step towards it. What you focus on with love will begin to take shape.

## Mulank 7 — Justice

*Ruled by Ketu*

**This week's energy:** Balance and truth are coming into focus. Old karmic threads may find gentle resolution this week.

**Guidance:** Be honest with yourself and fair with others. Offer your prayers to your ancestors and let go of what no longer needs carrying.

## Mulank 8 — Queen of Pentacles

*Ruled by Saturn · Shani*

**This week's energy:** You've been carrying a lot, and now it's time to nurture yourself the way you nurture everyone else. Comfort and abundance flow through care.

**Guidance:** Cook something nourishing, tend to your home, and rest well. Caring for yourself is also sacred work.

## Mulank 9 — Page of Pentacles

*Ruled by Mars · Mangal*

**This week's energy:** A new path of learning or a practical opportunity is opening up. Small, steady beginnings will grow into something solid.

**Guidance:** Channel your fire into patience. Learn, practise, and trust that consistent effort brings lasting rewards.
