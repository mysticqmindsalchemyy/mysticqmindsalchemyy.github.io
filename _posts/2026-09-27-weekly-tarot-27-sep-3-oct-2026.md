Your weekly guidance during the sacred days of **Pitru Paksha**. Find your Mulank below to discover your card for the week, the energy it brings, and gentle guidance for the days ahead.

![This week's tarot spread for Mulank 1 to 9](/assets/img/weekly/2026-09-27-spread.jpg)

## Mulank 1 — Four of Wands

*Ruled by Sun · Surya*

**This week's energy:** This week feels like coming home. The love and effort you've poured in is finally blossoming, so let yourself celebrate.

**Guidance:** Make space for the people you love, and hold your elders and ancestors close in your heart with gratitude.

## Mulank 2 — Knight of Wands

*Ruled by Moon · Chandra*

**This week's energy:** A lovely spark is lighting up inside you. You may feel braver, freer and ready for a fresh adventure.

**Guidance:** Follow your excitement, and give yourself a gentle pause before big choices. Your fire shines brightest when it's focused.

## Mulank 3 — The Magician

*Ruled by Jupiter · Guru*

**This week's energy:** Everything you need is already within you. This is a beautiful week to turn a heartfelt dream into something real.

**Guidance:** Set a loving intention and take one small step towards it. Your words carry a special power now, so share them kindly.

## Mulank 4 — The Empress

*Ruled by Rahu*

**This week's energy:** Life is gently nurturing you this week. The seeds you planted with love are beginning to bloom all around you.

**Guidance:** Be gentle with yourself and savour life's simple comforts. Your creativity flows most freely when you let things unfold.

## Mulank 5 — Justice

*Ruled by Mercury · Budh*

**This week's energy:** The universe is lovingly bringing things back into balance. Trust that honesty and fairness will lead you to the right outcome.

**Guidance:** Speak from the heart and choose what feels fair and true. The kindness in your choices this week will stay with you.

## Mulank 6 — Queen of Swords

*Ruled by Venus · Shukra*

**This week's energy:** Your heart and mind are finally speaking the same language. You're seeing your relationships with loving clarity.

**Guidance:** Share your truth softly and lovingly. Healthy boundaries are a beautiful way of caring for yourself and for others.

## Mulank 7 — Judgement

*Ruled by Ketu*

**This week's energy:** A soft inner calling is awakening within you. It's time to set down old burdens and step into a lighter, renewed you.

**Guidance:** Allow yourself to forgive and let go. During Pitru Paksha, a quiet prayer for your ancestors can bring your heart deep peace.

## Mulank 8 — Two of Cups

*Ruled by Saturn · Shani*

**This week's energy:** Beautiful connections surround you this week. Someone may reach out to you with warmth, understanding or support.

**Guidance:** Let others in and accept the support offered to you. You don't have to carry everything alone.

## Mulank 9 — Three of Pentacles

*Ruled by Mars · Mangal*

**This week's energy:** Your talents are shining, and others are noticing. Working hand in hand with others brings you both joy and recognition.

**Guidance:** Welcome the ideas and support of those around you. Together, you can create something truly lasting.
