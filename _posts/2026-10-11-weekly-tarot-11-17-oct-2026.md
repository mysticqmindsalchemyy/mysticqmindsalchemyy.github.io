Your weekly guidance for the sacred days of **Navratri**. Find your Mulank below to discover your card for the week, the energy it brings, and gentle guidance for the days ahead.

![This week's tarot spread for Mulank 1 to 9](/assets/img/weekly/2026-10-11-spread.jpg)

## Mulank 1 — The Moon

*Ruled by Sun · Surya*

**This week's energy:** Some things may feel unclear or emotionally foggy this week. Not everything is as it seems, and your mind may play tricks on you.

**Guidance:** Don't rush big decisions. Light a diya, trust your intuition, and let Maa's light reveal the truth in its own time.

## Mulank 2 — The Empress

*Ruled by Moon · Chandra*

**This week's energy:** The Divine Feminine is glowing through you this Navratri. Abundance, beauty and loving care are flowing into your life.

**Guidance:** Honour the Devi within you. Create, nurture, beautify your space, and open your heart to receive love.

## Mulank 3 — Nine of Wands

*Ruled by Jupiter · Guru*

**This week's energy:** You may feel tired, but you are much closer to the finish line than you think. Your resilience is your strength.

**Guidance:** Protect your energy and don't give up now. Ask for support where you need it, and keep going with faith.

## Mulank 4 — Three of Wands

*Ruled by Rahu*

**This week's energy:** Your plans are beginning to take shape, and new horizons are opening. Opportunities may come from far away.

**Guidance:** Think bigger and prepare well. Keep your eyes on the future and trust that your efforts are expanding.

## Mulank 5 — Ace of Wands

*Ruled by Mercury · Budh*

**This week's energy:** A brand-new spark of inspiration is arriving. Fresh energy, passion and a new beginning are blessing you.

**Guidance:** Act on that idea while the fire is bright. Navratri is an auspicious time to begin something new.

## Mulank 6 — Knight of Swords

*Ruled by Venus · Shukra*

**This week's energy:** Thoughts, news and events may move very fast this week. You may feel an urge to act or speak immediately.

**Guidance:** Speak your truth, but with kindness. Pause and breathe before reacting, so your words heal rather than hurt.

## Mulank 7 — The Hermit

*Ruled by Ketu*

**This week's energy:** Your soul is calling you inward. This is a deeply spiritual week, perfect for fasting, prayer and quiet reflection.

**Guidance:** Give time to your sadhana and to silence. The wisdom you seek is waiting in the stillness within.

## Mulank 8 — Three of Pentacles

*Ruled by Saturn · Shani*

**This week's energy:** Teamwork and recognition are highlighted. Your skills and steady effort are being noticed and valued.

**Guidance:** Collaborate, share ideas and be open to feedback. Together you will build something strong and lasting.

## Mulank 9 — Two of Swords

*Ruled by Mars · Mangal*

**This week's energy:** You may feel stuck between two choices, or be avoiding a decision you know needs to be made.

**Guidance:** Remove the blindfold and listen to your heart. Call on Maa Durga's courage and choose with clarity.

---

*Don't know your Mulank? Add the digits of your birth date until you get a single number. Born on the 23rd → 2 + 3 = Mulank 5.*
