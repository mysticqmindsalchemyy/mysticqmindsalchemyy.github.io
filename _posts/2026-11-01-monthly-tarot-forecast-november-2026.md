Your **monthly tarot forecast for November 2026**. Find your Mulank below to see the theme of your month and five cards: your overall energy, health, relationships, finance and career, and a message from your angels.

November 2026 is a **Universal Month 3**, the number of joy, creativity and self-expression, and it brings the light of Diwali. It is a month to speak your truth, celebrate what you have built, and let your inner light shine.

## Mulank 1 — Joy that Comes Home

*For everyone born on the 1st, 10th, 19th or 28th of any month*

**Theme of the month:** After months of pushing forward, November asks you to enjoy what you have built and the people who share it with you.

### Overall Energy — Ten of Cups

*Harmony · Family · Emotional Fulfilment*

This is one of the happiest cards in the deck. Expect warm moments with family, festive gatherings and a deep feeling that you belong. Sun-ruled Mulank 1 often leads from the front; this month, simply enjoy being part of the circle.

**Key tip:** Slow down and soak in the joy. You have earned it.

### Health — The Moon

*Intuition · Sleep · Emotional Waves*

Your energy may rise and fall with your moods. Pay attention to sleep and to worries that keep you awake at night. Your body is asking you to rest and listen, not to push through.

**Key tip:** Keep a calm bedtime routine, and note down what is on your mind before you sleep.

### Relationships — Queen of Swords

*Honesty · Clarity · Healthy Boundaries*

Clear, kind communication strengthens your bonds. Say what you mean, gently. Couples: an honest conversation brings you closer. Singles: you are drawn to people who are straightforward, and that is a good sign.

**Key tip:** Speak your truth with warmth, not sharpness.

### Finance & Career — Ace of Pentacles

*New Opportunity · Fresh Start · Prosperity*

A new opportunity, offer or source of income is on its way, perfect timing for the Diwali season. Plant the seed now and it can grow steadily.

**Key tip:** Say yes to practical opportunities, and start small but start.

### Angel Message — The Lovers

> “Choose with your heart, and stay true to your values.”

Your angels remind you that the right choices feel aligned inside, not just look good outside. Trust the connections and decisions that feel true to who you are.

*When your heart and values agree, you cannot go wrong.*

**Your November in one line:** “Enjoy the joy around you, rest your mind, speak with kind honesty, welcome a fresh opportunity, and choose what feels truly aligned.”
